## Photosynthesis (Biology 101)

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy in chloroplasts.
Two stages: light-dependent reactions (thylakoid membranes, produce ATP and NADPH)
and the Calvin cycle (stroma, fixes CO2 into glucose).

## Mitosis phases

Prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase. In metaphase chromosomes align at the
cell equator; in anaphase sister chromatids separate toward opposite poles.
